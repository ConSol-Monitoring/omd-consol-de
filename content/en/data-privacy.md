                <h2 id="storage-title">Cookies and browser storage</h2>
                <p>This website does not set cookies or use browser-based analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or embedded third-party services. Fonts, scripts, images and search are served from this website. External websites and videos open only when you follow a link.</p>
                <p>Project badges are downloaded from GitHub and Shields.io by our site builder and refreshed hourly. Your browser loads these cached images from this website without contacting the badge providers.</p>
                <p>We use your browser’s local storage for two preferences: <code>td-color-theme</code> remembers the light, dark or automatic theme you select; <code>monitoring-storage-notice-v1</code> remembers when you dismiss the storage notice. These values stay in your browser until you clear this website’s data. They are not sent to our server or used to identify you.</p>
                <p>The default theme follows your device settings without saving a preference. Dismissing the notice does not enable any additional storage or services. You can clear these preferences in your browser’s site-data settings; the site also works with browser storage disabled.</p>
                <h3>Server access logs</h3>
                <p>The web server records requests in access logs, including the IP address, request time, requested URL, response status, browser information and referring URL when supplied. GoAccess processes these logs on the server to report website usage. It does not add browser scripts, cookies or other browser storage.</p>
